Help and Support

  • We’re Here to Help

    • Having trouble with the app or have a question about volunteering? This page covers the most common questions and tells you how to reach us if you need more help.

  • Frequently Asked Questions

    • How do I create an account?

    Tap “Sign Up” on the welcome screen, enter your name, email, and password, then follow the steps to set up your profile. You can start browsing opportunities right away.

    I forgot my password. What do I do?

    Tap “Forgot Password” on the login screen and enter your email. We’ll send you a link to reset it. If you don’t see the email, check your spam folder.

    How do I sign up for a volunteer activity?

    Open the Opportunities tab, choose an activity you’re interested in, and tap “Join.” You’ll get a confirmation and a reminder before the event.

    Can I cancel my sign-up?

    Yes. Go to My Activities, select the event, and tap “Cancel Sign-Up.” Please cancel as early as you can so organizers can find someone to take your place.

    How do I update my profile?

    Go to your Profile page and tap “Edit Profile.” You can change your name, photo, contact details, and interests.

    How can my organization post an activity?

    Organizers can create an event from the Create Activity page by adding the title, date, location, description, and number of volunteers needed.

    I’m not getting notifications. How do I fix it?

    Check that notifications are turned on for the app in your phone’s settings. Then make sure notifications are enabled in the app’s Settings page.

  • Troubleshooting Tips
    1. Check your internet connection.
    2. Close and reopen the app.
    3. Update to the latest version of the app.
    4. Restart your device.
    5. Log out and log back in.
