About Us

  • Who We Are

    • Community Volunteer is a mobile app that connects people who want to help with the organizations and neighbors who need it. We believe every community is stronger when its members show up for one another, and we built this app to make that easier.

  • Our Mission

    • To make volunteering simple, accessible, and rewarding for everyone by bringing community needs and willing helpers together in one place.

  • Our Vision

    • A community where no one who needs help goes without it, and no one who wants to help is left wondering where to start.

  • What We Do

    • Help volunteers discover local events and opportunities that fit their interests and schedule Let organizations post activities, set up needed roles, and manage sign-ups Keep everyone informed with reminders and updates Track volunteer participation so people can see the difference they make

  • How It Works
    1. Create your account and set up your profile.
    2. Browse volunteer opportunities in your community.
    3. Sign up for the activities that matter to you.
    4. Show up, lend a hand, and make an impact.
  • Our Values

    • Compassion: We put people first.

    Community: Real change starts close to home.

    Inclusivity: Everyone has something to give, and everyone is welcome.

    Commitment: Small, consistent actions create lasting change.

  • Join Us

    • Whether you have an hour or a whole weekend, your time matters. Join Community Volunteer today and help build a stronger, kinder community.
